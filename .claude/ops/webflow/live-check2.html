How it works

From your bank feed to board-ready

  1. 01

    Connect

    We link your banks, invoicing tools and accounting software — QuickBooks, Xero, Zoho Books or ours. Migration handled for you.

  2. 02

    We do the daily work

    Every transaction is captured, sorted and matched against your bank — daily. Anything odd, duplicated or missing a receipt gets pulled out for a person to look at.

  3. 03

    Your accountant checks it

    A dedicated accountant goes through the work, sorts out anything that was set aside, and signs the filings. Nothing goes out on the software’s say-so alone.
